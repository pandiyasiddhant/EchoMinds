Questions, gently answered

Everything you might want to know about how EchoMinds works, how we protect your privacy, and how to bring it to your school.

General

EchoMinds is a calm, multilingual AI companion. Students can speak or write freely — in English or Hinglish — and receive a gentle Wellness Snapshot, while schools get a warm way to notice who might appreciate a check-in. It is built for educational and research purposes.

No. It's a gentle screening companion only. Every Wellness Snapshot should be reviewed by a caring, trained counsellor before any action is taken — EchoMinds never issues a clinical diagnosis.

Students can check in privately and see their own Progress Journey. Schools and counsellors get a warm, cohort-level view to notice trends and offer support where it's needed.

Privacy & Data

All entries are encrypted in transit and at rest. Only the student and authorised school staff (based on role) can access individual results — never third parties.

Schools see gentle wellness summaries, not necessarily every raw entry. Access is configured by your school's administrator, and always kept private.

No individual submissions are used without explicit, anonymised consent as part of our research partnerships.

Technology

Yes — our transformer model was fine-tuned specifically on code-mixed Hindi-English text collected from real student writing samples, so it understands natural code-switching.

Our latest model understands correctly about 94% of the time on held-out validation data. Every Wellness Snapshot also shows how clearly it understood you.

Under 2 seconds on average, from the moment you finish to your Wellness Snapshot appearing.
