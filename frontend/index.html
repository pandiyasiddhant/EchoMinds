What EchoMinds offers

Everything schools need to offer a little more support

A calm toolkit built around one goal — helping students feel heard, and helping the people who care about them notice a little sooner.

AI-Powered Understanding

A gentle, fine-tuned multilingual model listens to free-text writing and notices emotional patterns with care.

Supports Hinglish

Purpose-built for code-mixed Hindi-English writing the way students actually type.

Secure & Private

Fast, Gentle Feedback

Clear, Honest Insights

Wellness Dashboard for Schools

Progress Journey

Research Driven

How it works

Five gentle steps, whenever you're ready

From a quiet thought to genuine support — every step designed to feel easy.

1

Sign in

A calm, private space just for you — students and staff each get their own.

2

Speak or write

Share your thoughts by voice or text, in English or Hinglish — whatever feels natural.

3

AI listens gently

Our model quietly understands the emotion and tone behind your words.

4

Get your Wellness Snapshot

A clear, kind summary of how you're doing — never a score to worry about.

5

Receive gentle suggestions

Small, supportive next steps — picked just for how you're feeling today.
